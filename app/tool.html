The shortest walk stays outdoors; the coolest walk may go through the buildings you open. “No PennCard” opens only buildings that do not need a Penn ID; indoor passages are assumed open and air-conditioned. Weighted exposure counts a metre in the sun as 1 and a shaded metre as less, more so the farther you have walked (Wen et al. 2025).
